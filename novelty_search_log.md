# Gate-2 Half A — novelty search log

Method: focused web searches per component (12 queries, 2026-11-22),
aimed at prior art under other names, following the AQ6 lesson (the
factor-2 law was demoted by exactly such a find). Verdict categories
fixed in advance: (a) STATED / (b) IMPLICIT / (c) NOT FOUND.
**Boundary:** web-indexed literature and reviews reachable by these
queries; no paywalled full-text corpus, no exhaustive arXiv full-text
search. "Not found" is a bounded-search verdict, never proof of
novelty.

## Component (i) — kernel shrink for c_g = c

Searched: Ignatowski derivations and their assumption lists
(Pelissetto & Testa, "Getting the Lorentz transformations without
requiring an invariant speed"; Lévy-Leblond; Stepanov, "On simplified
axiomatic foundations of special relativity"; Koekoek, arXiv:2109.11925)
— the theorem and the unfixed sign/value of κ are (a) STATED, including
Pauli's remark (quoted by Stepanov) that nothing can be said about the
sign of the constant from the group-theoretic derivation. Weinberg's
soft-graviton universality (Weinberg 1964/65; reviewed in Porrati,
"Old and New No Go Theorems…", arXiv:1209.4876; "Constraints on
emergent gravity", arXiv:0904.0453) — (a) STATED. Direct searches for
an assembled premise-reduction of c_g = c (why gravity and light share
a cone, assumption audits, GW170817 theory explanations) returned
only single-metric-GR restatements and popular accounts — no premise
ledger, no Weinberg+Ignatowski assembly. **Verdict: components
(a) STATED; the assembled claim (c) NOT FOUND.**

## Component (ii) — erasure vs selection / tracking slope

Searched: 't Hooft technical naturalness (Snowmass white paper
arXiv:2205.05708; EPJC review 2023) — symmetry restoration at zero is
(a) STATED as what makes a small parameter special. Barbieri–Giudice
(1988) and descendants (Anderson & Castaño, hep-ph/9409419; sensitivity
criteria literature) — a derivative-of-output-w.r.t.-input criterion
is (a) STATED as a fine-tuning measure. Axion literature (PQ reviews;
quantum-simulation papers arXiv:2512.13049, arXiv:2603.12194) — the
minimum at θ_eff = 0 "independent of microscopic details" is
(a) STATED; "dynamically relaxes/cancels" is the standard verb.
Relaxion literature (Graham–Kaplan–Rajendran 2015; Nelson &
Prescod-Weinstein, arXiv:1708.00010) — its verb is "selected", and the
axion/relaxion contrast is discussed, but no quantitative
output-tracking criterion is drawn. Self-tuning / adjustment-mechanism
literature (Weinberg no-go; Cline & Firouzjahi hep-th/0207155; Fab
Four, arXiv:1112.4866) — input-independence as desideratum is
(a) STATED in that sector. Axion quality problem (arXiv:1911.09385;
PRL 134, 111803 (2025)) — the PQ-quality premise is (a) STATED under
that name. No source found states a tracking-slope (derivative of a
mechanism's output w.r.t. its inserted parameters / erased input) as
a binary erasure-vs-selection classifier applied across mechanism
classes with computed slopes. **Verdict: the distinction in substance
(b) IMPLICIT (widely, under scattered terminology); the
criterion-as-classifier (c) NOT FOUND.**
